# AnyCompany Remote Work Policy

Document owner: People Operations. Version 2.1, effective 1 March 2026. Applies to all AnyCompany employees in the United States whose role can be performed away from an AnyCompany office.

## 1. Purpose

AnyCompany is a hybrid company. This policy sets out how many days you may work remotely, which days teams meet in the office, how fully remote arrangements are approved and what equipment and allowances you receive for working from home.

## 2. Hybrid working: the default arrangement

- Employees may work remotely **up to 3 days per week**. The remaining working days are spent in your home office location (the AnyCompany office named in your employment record).
- **Tuesday is the company-wide anchor day**: every office-based and hybrid employee is expected in the office on Tuesdays, unless they are on leave, travelling for business or have an approved fully remote arrangement. Teams may agree one additional team anchor day each week.
- Remote days do not need to be the same days each week, but you must keep your calendar up to date so colleagues know where you are working.
- Your manager may ask you to come in on a remote day for events such as customer visits, onboarding a new team member or quarterly planning, with at least 2 working days' notice.
- New employees spend their **first 4 weeks** in the office full time to complete onboarding.

## 3. Core hours and availability

- Core collaboration hours are **10:00 to 15:00 in your home office's local time zone**. You must be reachable on company chat and video during core hours whether you are at home or in the office.
- Outside core hours you can arrange your working day flexibly, as long as you complete your contracted hours and meet your commitments.

## 4. Fully remote arrangements

- Working remotely more than 3 days per week on an ongoing basis is a **fully remote arrangement** and requires **approval from the Vice President of your function**, with a recommendation from your manager and a review by People Operations.
- Fully remote arrangements are **reviewed every 6 months**. The review considers team collaboration, performance and business needs, and the arrangement can be ended with 8 weeks' notice.
- Fully remote employees must live in a US state where AnyCompany is registered as an employer and must attend their team's quarterly in-person gathering (travel is paid by AnyCompany).

## 5. Working from another location

- Working from a different US state for **more than 30 days in a calendar year** requires prior approval from People Operations because of payroll tax and insurance implications.
- Working from outside the United States is limited to **20 days per calendar year**, requires approval from your manager and People Operations at least 4 weeks in advance, and is subject to the destination's immigration and data-protection rules.

## 6. Equipment and allowances

- AnyCompany provides a laptop, headset and security key to every employee.
- New hybrid and fully remote employees receive a **one-off home office stipend of 750 USD** for a desk, chair, monitor or similar equipment, claimed through the expense system within 90 days of joining or of the arrangement being approved.
- Hybrid and fully remote employees receive a **monthly internet and utilities allowance of 50 USD**, paid through payroll.
- Equipment bought with the stipend belongs to the employee; laptops and security hardware remain AnyCompany property and must be returned when you leave.

## 7. Health, safety and security at home

- Set up a safe workspace: a stable desk, a proper chair and good lighting. Report work-related injuries at home the same way as in the office.
- Connect through the company VPN, lock your screen when you step away and never use public Wi-Fi without the VPN. Confidential documents must not be printed at home unless your role has an approved exception.
- Household members must not use AnyCompany devices.

## 8. Changing or ending an arrangement

Hybrid schedules are agreed with your manager and can be adjusted at any time by mutual agreement. AnyCompany may change this policy with 8 weeks' notice. Disagreements about remote work arrangements are handled through your HR Business Partner.
