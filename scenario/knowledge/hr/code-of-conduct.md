# AnyCompany Code of Conduct

Document owner: People Operations and Legal. Version 4.0, effective 1 January 2026. Applies to all AnyCompany employees, contractors and board members.

## 1. Our values

AnyCompany is built on four values: **customer trust, candour, ownership and respect**. The Code of Conduct turns those values into expectations for how we behave with each other, with customers and partners, and with the public. Every employee reads and acknowledges the Code when joining and again every January.

## 2. Respect and inclusion

- We treat everyone with dignity. Harassment, bullying, discrimination and retaliation are prohibited, whether in the office, remotely, on company chat or at company events.
- Decisions about hiring, pay, promotion and work assignments are based on merit and business needs, never on protected characteristics such as race, religion, gender, age, disability, sexual orientation or national origin.
- Managers are responsible for the conduct of their teams and must act on concerns within 2 working days of becoming aware of them.

## 3. Speaking up

- If you see something that conflicts with this Code, raise it with your manager, your HR Business Partner, or the confidential **AnyCompany Speak Up line** (available 24 hours a day, anonymous reports accepted).
- Reports are acknowledged within 2 working days and investigated by People Operations or Legal. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is a serious violation that leads to dismissal.

## 4. Conflicts of interest

- Disclose any outside activity, investment or relationship that could affect your judgement at work, including a second job, a board seat, or a close relative who works for a customer, supplier or competitor.
- You may not approve expenses, pay, leave or contracts for a relative or partner. Tell People Operations if someone in your reporting line is a close relative.
- Outside employment requires written approval from your manager and must not use AnyCompany time, equipment or information.

## 5. Gifts, hospitality and anti-bribery

- You may accept gifts and hospitality from a business partner worth up to **100 USD per person per calendar year**. Anything above that value must be declined or approved in advance by your director and recorded in the gift register.
- Cash and cash equivalents (gift cards, vouchers) may never be accepted or offered, regardless of value.
- AnyCompany does not pay or accept bribes, kickbacks or facilitation payments anywhere in the world. Charitable donations and sponsorships on behalf of the company require Legal approval.

## 6. Confidential information and data protection

- Protect confidential information about AnyCompany, our customers and our colleagues. Share it only with people who need it to do their job, inside or outside the company.
- Personal data of employees and customers is processed only for the purpose it was collected for. Report any suspected data breach to security@anycompany.example **within 1 hour** of discovery.
- Confidential information remains confidential after you leave AnyCompany.

## 7. Using company systems and AI tools

- Company laptops, email, chat and cloud systems are for business use; reasonable personal use is allowed if it does not interfere with work or violate this Code.
- Use only software and online services approved by IT. **Do not paste confidential, personal or customer data into AI tools or services that AnyCompany has not approved.** Approved AI assistants are listed on the IT portal and are governed by the AI usage standard.
- Keep multi-factor authentication enabled, never share passwords and report lost devices to IT immediately.

## 8. Communicating about AnyCompany

- Only authorised spokespeople speak to the media, analysts or regulators on behalf of AnyCompany.
- On social media you may say you work for AnyCompany, but make clear that opinions are your own, never disclose confidential information and never speak for the company.

## 9. Health, safety and substance use

- Follow safety instructions in offices and at customer sites and report hazards. Alcohol may be served at approved company events; being impaired by alcohol or drugs while working is prohibited.

## 10. Accurate records

- Record time, expenses, leave and business transactions accurately and promptly. Never falsify, back-date or destroy records that are subject to a legal hold.

## 11. Consequences

Violations are investigated fairly. Outcomes range from coaching and written warnings to dismissal, and may be reported to the authorities where the law requires. Managers who ignore violations in their teams are themselves in breach of this Code.

## 12. Acknowledgement

All employees confirm in the HR portal each January that they have read and understood the Code. New employees acknowledge it within their first 2 weeks.
