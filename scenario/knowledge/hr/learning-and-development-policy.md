# AnyCompany Learning and Development Policy

Document owner: People Operations (Talent Development). Version 2.3, effective 1 January 2026. Applies to all AnyCompany employees in the United States.

## 1. Purpose

AnyCompany invests in every employee's growth. This policy describes the learning budget and learning days you receive each year, what they can be spent on, how to get approval and reimbursement, and the additional programmes available for certifications, tuition and mentoring.

## 2. Annual learning budget

- Every employee has a **learning budget of 1,500 USD per calendar year**, available from the first day of employment and pro-rated by month for employees who join during the year (125 USD per remaining month).
- The budget covers online and classroom courses, professional certifications and exam fees, conferences and workshops (registration fees), professional association memberships, and books or e-books related to your current role or your agreed development plan.
- The budget does not cover degree programmes (see section 6), equipment, software subscriptions used for day-to-day work, or travel and accommodation, which follow the travel and expense policy and are approved separately by your manager.
- **Unused budget does not roll over** into the next calendar year and is not paid out.

## 3. Learning days

- Every employee receives **5 paid learning days per calendar year**, in addition to annual leave. Learning days are for attending courses, conferences, study for a certification exam or structured self-study agreed with your manager.
- Learning days are booked with your manager at least 1 week in advance and recorded in the HR system as a learning day, not as annual leave. They can be taken as half days and do not carry over.
- Team learning events organised by AnyCompany, such as the annual engineering summit, do not count against your learning days.

## 4. Approval and reimbursement

- Request approval from your manager before you commit to spending. Your manager confirms that the activity is related to your role or development plan and that the budget is available. Requests up to **500 USD** can be approved by your manager alone; requests above 500 USD are also reviewed by Talent Development within 3 working days.
- Pay with your corporate card where possible. Otherwise, submit the expense in the expense system **within 30 days** with an itemised receipt and your manager's approval attached.
- Share what you learned: after a course or conference costing more than 500 USD, write a short summary or give a 15-minute team talk within one month.

## 5. Certifications

- Exam fees for professional certifications count against your learning budget. If you fail an exam, AnyCompany pays for **one retake** from a central fund, outside your personal budget.
- Employees who obtain an AnyCompany priority certification (the list is published on the learning portal each January) receive a **one-off recognition bonus of 500 USD**, paid in the following payroll.
- Renewal fees for certifications relevant to your role are reimbursed from your budget.

## 6. Tuition assistance

- Employees with at least **12 months of service** may apply for tuition assistance for accredited degree or diploma programmes relevant to a current or future role at AnyCompany.
- AnyCompany reimburses **up to 5,250 USD per calendar year** of tuition and mandatory fees on successful completion of each course (grade C or better, or pass).
- Tuition assistance requires approval from your director and Talent Development, and a **24-month retention agreement**: if you leave AnyCompany voluntarily within 24 months of a reimbursement, the amount is repaid pro rata.

## 7. Mentoring and internal programmes

- The **AnyCompany Mentoring Programme** pairs employees with a mentor from another team for 6 months. Two cohorts start each year, in February and September; apply through the learning portal.
- Managers new to AnyCompany complete the **Leading at AnyCompany** programme (3 days) within their first 6 months.
- All employees complete mandatory training in security awareness, the Code of Conduct and data protection each year; these courses do not use your learning budget or learning days.

## 8. Responsibilities

Employees own their development plan and discuss it with their manager at least twice a year. Managers protect learning time, approve requests promptly and make sure the budget is used fairly across the team. Talent Development publishes the priority certification list, runs the programmes in this policy and reports usage to the leadership team quarterly.
