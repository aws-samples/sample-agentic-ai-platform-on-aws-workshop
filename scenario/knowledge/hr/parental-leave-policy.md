# AnyCompany Parental Leave Policy

Document owner: People Operations. Version 1.4, effective 1 January 2026. Applies to all AnyCompany employees in the United States.

## 1. Purpose

AnyCompany supports employees who welcome a child through birth, adoption, surrogacy or long-term foster placement. This policy describes who is eligible, how much paid parental leave you receive, how it can be taken and how we support your return to work. It applies in addition to any state or federal leave entitlement; where the law gives you more, the law applies.

## 2. Eligibility

- You are eligible for paid parental leave after **12 months of continuous service** with AnyCompany on the expected date of birth or placement.
- Employees with less than 12 months of service may take up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected parental leave and may use accrued annual leave during that time.
- Full-time and part-time employees are eligible; part-time employees receive the same number of weeks at their contracted hours.

## 3. Entitlement

- **Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave.** The primary caregiver is the parent with the main responsibility for the child's care during the leave; a birth mother is always eligible as a primary caregiver.
- **Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave.**
- Pay is 100 percent of base salary. Benefits, equity vesting and annual leave accrual continue during paid parental leave.
- Multiple births or placements at the same time do not increase the entitlement. Each new child in a separate event starts a new entitlement.
- Where both parents work at AnyCompany, each takes their own entitlement; one is the primary and one is the secondary caregiver for the same child.

## 4. Timing and flexibility

- Parental leave must be taken **within 12 months** of the birth or placement.
- The leave can be taken as a single block or in **up to 2 blocks of at least 2 weeks each**. Primary caregivers who give birth start their leave no later than the date of birth.
- Up to 2 weeks of the entitlement may be taken before the expected date for medical appointments, travel or preparation.

## 5. How to request parental leave

- Tell your manager and People Operations at least **8 weeks before the expected start date** where possible. People Operations files the parental leave request on your behalf: it is longer than the 10 consecutive days the HR assistant can submit, so the assistant answers questions about your entitlement and eligibility but does not file it.
- Provide the expected date of birth or placement. For adoption or fostering, a placement letter from the agency is sufficient.
- People Operations confirms your eligibility and dates in writing within 5 working days. Parental leave is not subject to the normal 4-week notice rule for long absences and does not need director approval.

## 6. Returning to work

- You return to the same role, or an equivalent role with the same pay and grade.
- **Phased return**: during your first **4 weeks** back you may work **80 percent of your hours for 100 percent of your pay**.
- Nursing parents have access to a private room in every AnyCompany office and reasonable paid breaks.
- You may request a temporary change to your hybrid schedule for up to 6 months after your return; managers approve these requests unless there is a clear business reason not to.

## 7. Additional unpaid leave

After the paid entitlement is used, you may request **up to 12 additional weeks of unpaid parental leave**, approved by your manager and People Operations. Health benefits continue; annual leave does not accrue during unpaid leave.

## 8. Pregnancy loss and fertility treatment

Employees who experience pregnancy loss receive up to 10 days of paid leave. Employees undergoing fertility treatment may take up to 5 paid days per year for appointments, recorded as sick leave without a certificate.

## 9. Pay during leave and taxes

Parental leave pay is processed through normal payroll and is taxed as salary. Where a state paid family leave programme pays part of your salary, AnyCompany tops up the difference to 100 percent.
