# AnyCompany Leave Policy

Document owner: People Operations. Version 3.2, effective 1 January 2026. Applies to all AnyCompany employees in the United States. Questions go to people-ops@anycompany.example.

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how paid and unpaid leave works at AnyCompany: how much leave you earn, how it accrues, how to request it, how much notice you must give and what happens to unused days. Where a local law gives you more than this policy, the law applies.

## 2. Annual leave

- Full-time employees accrue **20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year**, which is 1.67 days for every completed month of service. Accrual starts on your first day; there is no waiting period before you can use it.
- Part-time employees accrue annual leave pro rata to their contracted hours (a 50 percent schedule earns 10 days).
- Annual leave is counted in working days. Through the HR assistant leave is booked in **whole days**; half days are booked in the HR portal.
- Leave is paid at your normal base salary. Annual leave continues to accrue while you are on paid leave of any type, including parental leave.

## 3. Carry-over

- You may carry **up to 5 unused annual leave days** into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by **31 March**; after that date they expire.
- Any unused balance above 5 days is forfeited on 31 December. AnyCompany does not pay out unused annual leave while you are employed, except where state law requires it.

## 4. Sick leave

- Every employee receives **10 paid sick days per calendar year**, available in full from the first day of employment and pro-rated for part-time schedules.
- Sick leave covers your own illness, injury or medical appointments and caring for a sick immediate family member.
- Tell your manager before the start of your working day, or as soon as you reasonably can, and record the absence with the HR assistant or the HR portal on the day.
- A medical certificate is required for absences of **more than 3 consecutive working days**.
- Sick days do not carry over and are not paid out. If you exhaust your sick leave, additional absence is taken from annual leave or as unpaid leave in agreement with People Operations.

## 5. Requesting leave

- Submit requests through the HR assistant or the HR portal. Every request starts as **pending** and is approved or declined by your manager; balances are deducted only when a request is approved.
- Requests are recorded in consecutive calendar days from the start date. Weekends and public holidays that fall inside the period are credited back to your balance when the request is approved.
- Notice periods:
  - **1 to 2 days**: at least 2 working days' notice.
  - **3 to 10 consecutive days**: at least 2 weeks' notice.
  - **More than 10 consecutive days**: at least 4 weeks' notice, and the request **requires director approval** in addition to your manager's approval. The HR assistant does not submit requests longer than 10 days: ask your manager to file them with People Operations, who obtain the director's approval.
- Managers respond within 3 working days. A request that is still pending 3 working days before the start date is escalated to the department head.
- A pending request can be withdrawn at any time. Approved leave can be changed only with your manager's agreement.

## 6. Public holidays

AnyCompany observes the 11 US federal holidays each year. When a holiday falls on a Saturday it is observed on the preceding Friday; when it falls on a Sunday it is observed on the following Monday. Employees who are required to work on an observed holiday receive a day in lieu to be taken within 3 months.

## 7. Unpaid leave

Once annual leave is exhausted, employees with at least 12 months of service may request **up to 30 days of unpaid leave per calendar year** with the approval of their manager and a director. Benefits continue during unpaid leave; annual leave does not accrue.

## 8. Other paid leave

- **Bereavement**: 5 paid days for an immediate family member, 2 days for other relatives.
- **Jury duty**: paid for the duration of service on presentation of the summons.
- Parental leave is covered by the separate AnyCompany Parental Leave Policy.

## 9. Leaving AnyCompany

Accrued but unused annual leave is paid out in the final pay cheque. Negative balances (leave taken in advance of accrual) are deducted from the final pay where the law permits.

## 10. Record keeping

Balances shown by the HR assistant are updated nightly from the HR system. If you believe your balance is wrong, contact People Operations; corrections are made within 5 working days.
