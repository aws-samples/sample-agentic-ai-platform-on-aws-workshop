# AnyCompany Travel and Expense Policy

Document owner: Finance (Accounts Payable). Version 5.1, effective 1 January 2026. Applies to all AnyCompany employees and contractors who incur business expenses.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out which business expenses AnyCompany reimburses, the limits that apply, what evidence you must keep and how quickly you must submit a claim. Finance may decline claims that do not follow this policy.

## 2. General principles

- Spend company money as carefully as you would spend your own. Choose the lowest reasonable cost that meets the business need.
- Expenses must be approved by your manager. Managers may not approve their own expenses or those of a relative.
- Use your AnyCompany corporate card wherever it is accepted. Personal cards are reimbursed through the expense system.

## 3. Meals while travelling

- Meals on business trips are reimbursed up to a **daily limit of 60 USD per person**, covering breakfast, lunch and dinner together. Amounts above the limit are not reimbursed unless a director approves an exception in writing before the trip.
- The daily meal limit is reduced by half on travel days shorter than 8 hours and when a meal is included in a conference, hotel or flight.
- Alcohol is not reimbursed, except as part of approved client entertainment (section 6).
- Tips up to 20 percent are reimbursed as part of the meal.

## 4. Receipts

- An itemised receipt is required for **every expense above 25 USD**. For expenses of 25 USD or less, a receipt is recommended but not required if the expense is described clearly.
- Credit card statements alone are not accepted as receipts. Photograph paper receipts with the expense app on the day; originals can be discarded once the claim is approved.
- Missing receipts above 25 USD require a signed missing-receipt declaration and are limited to 3 per employee per year.

## 5. Submission deadline

- Submit expenses in the expense system **within 30 days of the date the expense was incurred** (for trips, within 30 days of the return date).
- Claims submitted between 31 and 90 days need director approval and an explanation. Claims older than **90 days are not reimbursed**.
- Finance pays approved claims with the next payroll run, at least twice a month.

## 6. Client entertainment

Entertaining customers or partners requires advance approval from your director. The limit is 120 USD per person including drinks, and the names and companies of all attendees must be listed on the claim.

## 7. Travel

- Book flights, trains and hotels through the AnyCompany travel tool at least 14 days in advance where possible.
- Flights are economy class; premium economy is allowed for flights longer than 6 hours, business class only with VP approval.
- Hotel limits are **250 USD per night** in New York, San Francisco, Boston, Seattle and Washington DC, and **180 USD per night** elsewhere in the United States, excluding taxes.
- Mileage for personal vehicles is reimbursed at the current IRS standard rate; parking and tolls are reimbursed with receipts. Rental cars are mid-size or smaller.
- Rideshares and taxis are reimbursed for business travel; personal commuting is not.

## 8. Not reimbursable

Personal entertainment, minibar items, traffic fines, airline upgrades without approval, personal travel insurance, spouse or family travel, and expenses already covered by a per diem or an allowance.

## 9. Compliance

Finance audits a sample of claims every month. Claims that breach this policy are declined, and deliberate misuse is handled under the Code of Conduct and may result in dismissal.
